## Rules

- No claim enters the abstract without a ledger row.
- No figure enters the paper without named source runs.
- Mark status as `blocked` when a baseline, ablation, or rerun is missing.
- Use status `candidate`, `mechanism-evidence`, `controlled-result`, `robust-result`, or `paper-ready`.
- Keep negative and indeterminate results visible.
- Do not promote a performance claim if required lifecycle work is outside the measured boundary.
